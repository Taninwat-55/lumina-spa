The ritual

One bathhouse on the harbour. Three rooms, one rhythm: cold, heat, rest.

No music and no screens. You hear the water under the deck and the stove ticking as it warms. Go round as many times as you like. Most people stop after three.

01 Cold 02 Heat

9°C

Nine degrees. Your breath goes short, then slow.

Then heat. Eighty-five degrees, and the harbour goes quiet.

03 Rest

Then you rest. That is the whole ritual.

Prototype: the hero and the passage from cold to heat. Rooms, the place and booking come next.

Lumina is a design concept. The bathhouse does not exist.
