Rooms and prices

Three rooms, one rhythm.

01 Cold

The plunge

The harbour itself. A ladder goes down from the deck into three metres of clear, salty water. Go in slowly or all at once. Either way, your breath settles after the first ten seconds.

Water
2–18 °C, by season
Depth
3 m, ladder from the deck
Stay
30 seconds to 2 minutes

02 Heat

The sauna

Wood-fired and lined with aspen, with a glass wall onto the water. Every hour on the half hour, the sauna master pours water on the stones, a saunagus, and the heat rolls down from the top bench.

Heat
80–90 °C
Seats
12, on three benches
Saunagus
Every hour at :30

03 Rest

The rest room

Wool blankets, low chairs and windows that face west over the harbour. There is tea, water and rye crisps. Nobody talks much in here, and nobody needs to.

On the table
Tea, water, rye crisps
Bring
Nothing; robes are here
Sound
The harbour, mostly
