01

Getting there

Cycle south along the harbour path from the city centre. It takes about twelve minutes, and there are racks at the pier gate. The harbour bus stops four minutes away on foot.

Walk to the end of the pier. The three shingled huts are ours: the sauna, the changing room and the rest room. The ladder into the water is on the far side.

04

House rules

  1. Shower before you go in.
  2. Speak softly in the sauna.
  3. Phones stay in the locker.
  4. Swim from the ladder only. The water is three metres deep.
  5. Leave the bench as you found it.
